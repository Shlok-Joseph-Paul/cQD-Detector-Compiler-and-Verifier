# Liu, Afzal, and Liu source notes

Approved for publication September 23, 2026: three papers, seven device variants, and 21 measurement rows. All rows remain pending scientific review and excluded from performance comparisons. User publication approval does not resolve the source conflicts.

The main papers are DOI 10.1002/advs.202205879 (Liu 2023, online 2022), 10.1038/s41598-020-80640-3 (Afzal 2021), and 10.1039/c5tc00673b (Liu 2015). Afzal's publisher supplement was checked; Liu 2023's supplement was unavailable, and Liu 2015's supplement availability was unconfirmed.

`held-candidates.json` preserves conflicting D* claims, spectral bounds, the PC61BM device's LDR >100 dB, modeled transit times, and undigitized graph series. These are source evidence, not additional measurement rows. `consistency-audit.json` compares reported R and EQE using the standard conversion without replacing either published value.

Twelve D* rows comprise four measured-noise records and eight shot-noise estimates. Three measured-noise rows with responsivity have unverified frequency matching. The D*-only control row is green under the current schema but remains pending review; this flag does not independently validate it. Nine performance-only rows have no D*/noise flags.

No missing bias, temperature, wavelength, noise instrument, or device area has been filled by assumption. The explicitly reported timing-device area remains in the notes because its match to the spectral specimen is unconfirmed. The 2015 theoretical timing estimates are excluded from measured speed, and LDR >100 dB is not converted into an equality.
