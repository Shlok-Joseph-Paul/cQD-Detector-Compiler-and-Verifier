# Perovskite trio source notes

Approved for publication on September 22, 2026. The atlas includes three papers, seventeen device variants and thirteen provisional measurement rows from Shen 2017, Li 2019 and Shen 2016. All thirteen rows remain `pending_review`, as approved, and are excluded from performance comparisons until scientific questions are resolved.

The twelve timing candidates in `measurements.wavelength-held.csv` lack a securely assigned primary wavelength. Their values, definitions, limitations and source locations are published in the corresponding device notes. They are preserved here as structured source evidence, not valid canonical measurement rows. A missing wavelength must not be replaced by an assumed excitation source, band edge or unrelated spectral peak.

`held-candidates.json` preserves the Shen 2017 detectivity lower bound and other limitations. The bound is also visible in the paper notes. It is not represented as an equality D* point. None of these source notes is an independent scientific validation of the published claims.
